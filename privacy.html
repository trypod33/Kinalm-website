Kinalm

Privacy Policy & Terms of Reference

Last updated: October 2026 · Applies to Kinalm CareTracker (com.kinalm) for Android.

1. Overview

Kinalm is a personal health, vital signs, nutrition, and caregiving record-keeping app built with a privacy-first, offline-first approach. It works without mandatory account registration, without central cloud servers, and without transmitting your personal or medical care data off your device.

2. Data you enter in the app

Kinalm processes and stores only the information you voluntarily enter, including vitals and health metrics (blood pressure, heart rate, blood sugar, A1C, blood oxygen, sleep, water intake, weight, mood, symptom notes); nutrition and dietary tracking (calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, sugar, sodium, cholesterol, meal items, custom goals and food overrides); medication management (names, dosages, schedules, intake logs, pill counts, refill thresholds, prescriber details); caregiving records (habits, tasks, care notes, behavior and meltdown logs, growth and milestone tracking, appointments, provider contacts, weekly summaries); and profile customization (local profile names, colors, display preferences, optional local PINs).

3. Default local storage and encryption

All records, logs, custom food overrides, and settings are saved strictly on your device inside an encrypted local database (SQLCipher). There is no remote server or cloud account system for your health records. Your data leaves your device only when you export an encrypted backup, share a report, or enable one of the optional connections below.

4. Nutrition and food lookups (third-party food databases)

When you search for a food item or scan a product barcode, Kinalm sends only that search text or barcode number to public nutrition databases, currently USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts, to fetch nutrition numbers. These requests contain no account information, no name, no email, no device identifier, no location, and none of your health records. Results are stored only on your device. Foods saved as custom overrides are checked first, so an online lookup only happens for items you search for.

5. Google Calendar sync (optional)

Kinalm can connect to the Google Calendar account configured on your device so appointments and events appear in both the app and your calendar. This is optional and stays off until you enable it. Synced event data passes through your device calendar system under the account you connected; Google's own privacy practices govern that account. You can disconnect the calendar at any time in Settings.

6. Optional contact and update communications

Kinalm offers an optional contact and updates feature for release news or support. It is purely voluntary. If you submit a request, we receive only the email address and preferences you explicitly submit via your device email app. That contact info is never linked to your in-app health entries, vitals, nutrition records, or caregiving logs.

7. On-device Assistant API (optional)

Kinalm can run a small helper server on your own device so a local assistant app you pair with it (such as Friday) can read and write entries directly. It is off by default, listens only on the device itself (127.0.0.1, never the network), and is protected by a pairing token shown only to you in Settings. This feature does not transmit any data to a remote server.

8. What we do not collect

We do not collect, monitor, track, or sell: your name, address, or phone number; precise GPS location data; advertising IDs or cross-app usage telemetry; or your in-app health measurements, vitals, nutrition entries, or medication records.

9. Third-party services and analytics

Kinalm does not bundle ad networks, telemetry trackers, or analytics SDKs. The only third-party network calls the app makes are the nutrition and barcode lookups described in Section 4, and those carry only your search text or a barcode number.

10. Backup and export security

Backups are encrypted with AES using a passphrase of your choice. We never store or access your backup files or encryption keys. You are responsible for storing backups in a safe location.

11. Device permissions

Permissions enable device-level features only and are never used to harvest or send your private logs: notifications and exact alarms (medication, habit, task, and appointment reminders); camera (scanning product barcodes, processed on the device); calendar access (optional Google Calendar sync); activity recognition and body sensors (step counts and related entries you log); and storage access (encrypted backup export and import).

12. Your rights and choices

Because all data resides locally on your device, you can modify, export, or delete your health records at any time within Kinalm, or by clearing the app data in your Android system settings.

13. Contact
